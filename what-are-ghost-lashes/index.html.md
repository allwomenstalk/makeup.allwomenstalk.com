# What Are Ghost Lashes? The No\-Mascara Look, Explained

Ghost lashes are bare eyelashes with no mascara, or just the tiniest touch on the tips and outer corners. Despite the name, this is a soft no-mascara makeup look, not white Halloween lashes. It lets skin, cheeks, or lips take the spotlight. You can skip mascara completely, use clear gel, or make a quiet compromise with brown mascara.

## What are ghost lashes?

The look leaves lashes close to their natural color and shape. [Cosmopolitan traces the “ghost” label](https://www.cosmopolitan.com/style-beauty/beauty/a71943866/ghost-lashes-zendaya/) to the idea of barely visible “ghost layers” in hair, and points to Zendaya's mascara-free makeup at _The Odyssey_ premiere. That example brought attention to the look; it does not mean she invented going without mascara.

Makeup artist Ernesto Casillas told Cosmopolitan that his focus for Zendaya was fresh, luminous skin, with warmth on the cheeks and elsewhere the sun would touch. For fall, makeup artist Dani Parkes [told Cosmopolitan](https://www.cosmopolitan.com/style-beauty/beauty/a73774176/fall-makeup-trends-2026/) she likes bare lashes with a bold lip. That is one appealing pairing, not a rule that mascara is suddenly wrong.

## How to wear the look

- **Start with the skin you like.** A light base or bare skin both work. You do not need to cover every mark to skip mascara.

- **Brighten only where you want it.** Makeup artist Hannah Murray [told Elle](https://www.elle.com/beauty/a73339691/no-mascara-trend-summer-2026/) she uses a little light-reflective or correcting concealer, kept away from the lower lashes, to keep the eye area fresh.

- **Curl if you enjoy the lift.** Murray suggests curling and, optionally, adding clear mascara for hold and shine. Use a clean curler gently, without tugging.

- **Put color somewhere else.** A cheek flush, a soft eye wash, or a [bold lip](https://makeup.allwomenstalk.com/the-right-way-to-wear-sexy-matte-lips/) gives the face a focal point. Murray likes a little cream shadow; Casillas used warm blush on Zendaya.

Our [no-mascara guide](https://makeup.allwomenstalk.com/how-to-look-good-without-mascara/) has more ways to play with the balance.

## What is a “kiss of mascara”?

It is Parkes's very small amount of mascara on the lash tips and outer corners, instead of a full coat from root to tip. Murray suggests wiping excess product from a brown-mascara wand before applying it. Try that if bare lashes feel too quiet on your face. A clear formula reads even softer; no product at all is the simplest version.

## Can you add false lashes?

Elle's experts mention light clusters or individual lashes as a subtle variation, but those are optional and change the fully bare effect. If you choose them, follow adhesive instructions, keep glue out of the eye, never share eye products, and remove them gently. The [FDA advises stopping an eye cosmetic immediately if it irritates you](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/eye-cosmetic-safety) and seeing a doctor if irritation persists. There is no reason to use adhesive just to participate in a trend.

## What if my lashes are light, short, or sparse?

The point is a bare look, so it will read differently on different people. You can leave lashes exactly as they are, add a little brown mascara, or shift attention to a lip or cheek. There is no “correct” lash density. Our [lash styling tips](https://makeup.allwomenstalk.com/tricks-to-make-your-eyelashes-look-longer-and-fuller/) offer more options if definition is what you want. Be cautious about dye or tint near your eyes; ask a qualified professional about safe options rather than trying an unverified home method.

## Ghost lashes versus Halloween white lashes

Ghost lashes mean little or no visible mascara. White lashes for a costume are a different makeup effect; our [Halloween makeup ideas](https://makeup.allwomenstalk.com/halloween-makeup-looks-for-girls-who-love-it/) are the place to browse that mood.

## FAQ

### Are ghost lashes just no mascara?

Usually, yes. Bare lashes are the clearest version. Some makeup artists also count curled lashes with clear gel or a tiny amount of brown mascara as a softer variation. The shared idea is that lashes do not dominate the makeup.

### What is a “kiss of mascara”?

It means applying only a little product to the tips and outer corners for gentle definition. Makeup artist Dani Parkes described that version to Cosmopolitan. Wiping excess from the wand first can help you keep it subtle.

### Should I use clear or brown mascara?

Choose by the effect you want. Clear mascara adds a little shine and hold with minimal visible pigment; a light touch of brown gives more definition. Neither is required. Try the look bare first, then add only what you miss.

### Can I wear false lashes with this look?

You can, but they are optional and may make the result less “ghost.” Elle's makeup experts suggest subtle clusters or individuals. Follow adhesive and removal directions carefully, and avoid any product that irritates your eyes.

### Are ghost lashes the same as white Halloween lashes?

No. “Ghost” here means barely visible, naturally bare lashes. White costume lashes are an intentionally conspicuous effect. The two looks share a word, not a technique.
