# A Beginner Friendly Way to Start Trying Matte Lipsticks

If you've been searching for the perfect formula to elevate your makeup game, finding a lipstick that provides a bold finish without drying out your lips can feel like an endless quest. Fortunately, the beauty world has delivered some amazing new options. [Charlotte Tilbury](https://www.charlottetilbury.com) released a new line of lipsticks that are meant to keep you looking fashionable without drying out your pretty lips. They each cost $32, which is a low price for something so innovative. Our friends at [Glamour](https://www.glamour.com/story/charlotte-tilbury-matte-revolution-lipstick) brought our attention to some of the most gorgeous lip colors in the new line, and here they are:

![Charlotte Tilbury Matte Revolution Lipstick Shades](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/x7/uz/la42s_color_face_brown_beauty_eye_600x1849.jpg)

Here are a few reasons why these matte lipsticks stand out from the crowd:

- **3D Glowing Pigments**: Formulated to make lips look wider, fuller, and lit from within.
- **Long-Lasting Hydration**: Keeps lips soft and moisturized, unlike traditional drying matte formulas.
- **Square-Angled Tip**: Mimics a lip brush for precise, mess-free application every time.

If you want to achieve the perfect pout, it's also helpful to follow a few simple [tips on how to apply matte lipstick perfectly](https://makeup.allwomenstalk.com/tips-on-how-to-apply-matte-lipstick-perfectly/). Prepping your lips with a gentle scrub and liner will ensure that these gorgeous shades look flawless all day long. Investing in high-quality lip products from the [best lipstick brands](https://makeup.allwomenstalk.com/best-lipstick-brands-to-add-to-your-makeup-bag/) is an easy way to elevate your daily beauty routine.

To complete your overall makeup look, you can pair these gorgeous matte shades with a classic smoky eye. For instance, the [Paris Color Riche Le Stylo Smoky](https://www.lorealparisusa.com/makeup/eye/eyeliner) in Avant Azure or the Color Riche Le Stylo shadow Amour is a fantastic option. Incorporating a product like the Color Riche Stylo Smoky Avant Azure or the Paris Color Riche Stylo Avant Azure allows you to easily create a sultry eye look that highlights your features, making it a perfect match for these Charlotte Tilbury lipsticks. You can find these lipsticks on the [official Charlotte Tilbury product page](https://www.charlottetilbury.com/us/products/makeup/lips/lipstick) or at major beauty retailers like Sephora.

Quick tips for perfect matte lips:

- **Exfoliate first**: Gently buff away dry skin before applying.
- **Line your lips**: Define your shape and prevent feathering.
- **Blot and layer**: Apply one coat, blot with a tissue, and apply a second coat for maximum longevity.
