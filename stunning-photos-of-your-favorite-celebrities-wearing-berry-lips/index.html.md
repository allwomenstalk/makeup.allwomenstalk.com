# 31 Stunning Photos of Celebrities Wearing Berry Lips

Berry-colored lips can elevate your look any time of the year. The color is chic, sophisticated, and exudes effortless confidence. Whether you are aiming for a subtle flush or a dramatic dark berry red lipstick, this versatile family of shades works wonders across every complexion. Now that autumn has arrived, you're likely to see these delicious berry lipstick shades worn by your favorite stars much more often. The deep hue looks incredibly flattering in colder weather and adds instant glam to any outfit. From vibrant raspberry to deep plum, let's take a look at 31 of Hollywood's loveliest celebrities wearing this timeless lip trend.

- **Raspberry & Cranberry:** Best for fair undertones seeking a vibrant pop of color.

- **Rich Plum & Mulberry:** Ideal for olive and medium skin tones wanting balanced depth.

- **Deep Burgundy & Blackberry:** Perfect for dark skin celebs or anyone craving a statement vampy lip.

| Berry Lip Shade | Best Skin Tone | Recommended Finish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bright Raspberry | Fair to Light | Satin or Glossy |
| Classic Plum | Medium to Olive | Matte or Cream |
| Dark Burgundy | Deep to Dark | Velvet Matte |
