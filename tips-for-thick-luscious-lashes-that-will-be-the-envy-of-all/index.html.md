# 7 Tips for Thick, Luscious Lashes: How to Get That Girl Crush Lash Look

I am always collecting tips for thick luscious lashes and have found some of the best ways to get that coveted girl crush lash look. Beautiful eyelashes can make your whole face appear lovelier, brighter, and more expressive. Whether you want a natural flutter or full-volume glam, these simple daily habits will elevate your natural lash routine. Use these tips for thick luscious lashes every day to protect your natural growth and boost volume without damage.

## 1. Use Eye Makeup Remover

One of the best tips for thick luscious lashes I can give you is to use eye makeup remover when removing your eye makeup. Your eyelashes are fragile, so it is best to remove your eye makeup with dedicated eye makeup remover rather than using your standard facial cleanser. Eye makeup remover is specially formulated to dissolve stubborn mascara gently without harsh tugging. Cleanser is designed to remove makeup and impurities from your skin rather than your delicate eyelashes. Using the correct product, such as a gentle micellar water or dual-phase cleanser from trusted brands like [Bioderma](https://www.bioderma.us), makes all the difference in maintaining lash length and health.

## 2. Treat Your Lashes Gently

Eyelashes are delicate and need careful handling, though we sometimes forget that during a busy evening routine. We can treat them gently by choosing the right product and applying it with care rather than rough scrubbing. Another way to treat your lashes gently is to apply your mascara with a light touch rather than dragging the bristles through them forcefully. If you curl your eyelashes, limit yourself to the minimum number of squeezes. Overusing an eyelash curler or pressing too hard can cause severe breakage. If you frequently struggle with leftover product from [sleeping with makeup on](#3), switching to a lighter touch during cleaning will protect your natural lash line.

- Handle lashes gently during cleansing and makeup removal
- Limit eyelash curler pressure to prevent mechanical damage
- Incorporate nourishing eyelash primers into your prep routine

## 3. Never Sleep with Makeup on

You should never sleep with makeup on, and you should especially never sleep with your mascara on. Mascara hardens your lashes and makes them stiff, which leaves them far more susceptible to snapping against your pillow. If you want to achieve full, luscious lashes, you cannot afford to lose individual hairs to avoidable habits that cause breakage. If you are absolutely too exhausted to stumble to the bathroom after a long day, keep a package of facial cleansing wipes on your nightstand. While not a permanent replacement for water and cleanser, wipes ensure you never go to sleep with stiff mascara on your eyes.

## 4. Use an Eyelash Primer

Eyelash primer can take your eyelashes from simple to stunning. Eyelash primer is a conditioning formula that you apply to your eyelashes with a wand right before mascara. It coats each individual lash to build volume and smooth out the texture. When you apply your mascara over top, the color has a rich base to grab onto, creating a fuller effect with fewer coats. Pairing a good primer with proper cleansing techniques like using an [eye makeup remover](#1) ensures your lashes stay strong while looking noticeably thicker.

## 5. Find Your Perfect Mascara

Aren't we all on the quest for the perfect mascara? Finding the right formula makes a huge impact on your daily beauty look. The key to a successful search is knowing exactly what your natural lashes need. Do you want a lengthening formula, a volumizing formula, or one that defines and separates every hair? Reading reviews online or browsing curated top-rated options on [Sephora](https://www.sephora.com) will give you helpful inside info on what works best for your specific lash type.

| Formula Type | Primary Benefit | Ideal Lash Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lengthening | Extends tips | Short or stubby lashes |
| Volumizing | Adds density | Thin or fine lashes |
| Defining | Prevents clumping | Uneven or crisscrossed lashes |

## 6. Stay Away from Waterproof Mascara

Waterproof mascara is a great product for specific occasions where you need long wear, such as weddings or outdoor summer events. However, you should limit wearing waterproof formulas to those special times because daily use can dry out your eyelashes. Waterproof mascara adheres tightly and is difficult to dissolve, requiring extra friction to rub away. This added friction can cause accidental eyelash loss. Unless necessary, focus on [finding your perfect mascara](#5) in a high-quality washable formula for daily wear. When you do wear waterproof formulas, press a saturated cotton pad against your eyes for several seconds before gently wiping.

## 7. Add Falsies for Special Occasions

Falsies are a gorgeous way to kick your look up a notch. While applying strip lashes daily can feel tedious, they are an excellent option to pull out for events, photos, or nights out. They come in a wide variety of styles, from natural wispy clusters to dramatic full strips, allowing you to choose your desired level of glam. You can explore high-quality lash styles from beauty retailers like [Ulta Beauty](https://www.ulta.com). It is best to wear false eyelashes only occasionally, as removing the lash glue regularly can pull on natural hairs. Following the gentle care steps listed above will help keep your natural lashes healthy and full.

There are a lot of little tricks you can use to get the full, luscious girl crush lash look you desire. Taking small steps every day to clean, prime, and protect your eyelashes pays off in long-term health and volume. What favorite eyelash tricks do you rely on for your daily routine?
