# How to Match Your Makeup to Your Outfit: 9 Celebrity Style Rules

Beauty is definitely in the eye of the beholder, but while makeup trends come and go, knowing how to match makeup to outfit choices remains a timeless skill. Whether you are aiming for a subtle eyeshadow look or a bold lip color, coordinating your palette with your clothing can earn you serious style points. Should eyeshadow match your outfit every time? Not necessarily verbatim, but creating harmony between your attire and cosmetics elevates your entire aesthetic. If you are wearing a classy and elegant outfit, your beauty look should share that same elevated feel.

When learning how to match makeup with outfit tones, keep these core principles in mind:

- **Highlight One Feature:** If your outfit is vibrant or intricate, let either your eyes or lips take center stage.

- **Harmonize Undertones:** Pair cool-toned garments with silver or icy shades, and warm clothing with gold, bronze, or peach tones.

- **Embrace Contrast or Monochrome:** You can either lean into a seamless [monochromatic beauty look](#2) or pick a complementary accent shade.

Take inspiration from how these nine stylish celebrities masterfully coordinate their makeup and wardrobe for every occasion:

## 1. Elle Fanning

![Elle Fanning](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/mg/m3/nckc7jn0_600x900.jpg)

Elle took her red-carpet presentation to the next level by coordinating both her eyeshadow and lipstick with the soft romantic hues of her floral gown. Matching makeup to outfit motifs like florals requires a delicate touch; picking up the subtle blush and petal tones keeps the ensemble cohesive without looking overdone.

## 2. Emma Stone

![Emma Stone](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/n9/vp/uw9typ5l_600x900.jpg)

Pretty in peach! Emma's [monochromatic beauty look](#2) demonstrates how matching warm eyeshadow and blush directly to a peach gown creates a glowing, unified appearance. Monochromatic styling works exceptionally well for fair skin tones and softer garment hues.

## 3. Katy Perry

![Katy Perry](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/v3/l0/z0imntbu_600x900.jpg)

Katy matched her sparkling dress with a playful sweep of green and purple eyeshadow. If you want to recreate rich jewel-toned eye makeup, using high-impact formulas like [L'Oréal Paris](https://www.lorealparisusa.com) eye shadow pencils or smoky stylo liners in shades like eternal blue or emerald can make your eyes pop while pulling out the shimmering accent colors in your dress.

## 4. Selena Gomez

![Selena Gomez](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/b7/j9/t6bcd4jz_600x900.jpg)

Selena went for a moody, sophisticated vibe with a deep [oxblood red lip stain](#4) that perfectly complemented her dark gown. When choosing makeup for a wine dress or dark evening wear, a vampy lip creates a striking focal point that anchors your overall attire.

## 5. Emmy Rossum

![Emmy Rossum](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/v0/fm/qxx7bqlx_600x900.jpg)

Emmy sure knows how to bring attention to those big brown eyes! When styling classy makeup for a red dress or warm-toned outfit, framing brown eyes with defined liner and soft metallic shadow creates a captivating contrast that enhances your natural facial structure.

## 6. Joan Smalls

![Joan Smalls](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/rv/er/qe05je12_600x900.jpg)

When done right, coordinating dark makeup elements can steer an all-black look into effortless chic territory and out of overly gothic style. Joan pairs crisp cat-eye liner with glowing skin to keep a dark ensemble feeling fresh, sleek, and high-fashion.

## 7. Lupita Nyong'o

![Lupita Nyong'o](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/an/vy/theqp790_600x900.jpg)

Lupita knows how to make vibrant turquoise work hard for a jaw-dropping effect. Wondering how makeup that matches green dress or blue attire can stand out? Applying a bright pop of turquoise liner or lid color echoes the garment's tone while celebrating bold color play according to expert fashion guidelines on [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com).

## 8. Taylor Swift

![Taylor Swift](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/yc/vv/neimcsu0_600x850.jpg)

You can never go wrong with a classic [bold red lip](#8) to perfectly match a fiery red dress. When seeking makeup to match red dress ensembles, pairing a blue-based crimson lipstick with clean, defined winged liner offers an iconic, timeless look that flatters virtually every skin tone.

## 9. Jennifer Lawrence

![Jennifer Lawrence](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/du/r3/qcmvkj8s_600x932.jpg)

J.Law was a golden goddess on the red carpet at [The Hunger Games](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1951261/) movie premiere. Her warm metallic eyeshadow and bronzed cheekbones paired seamlessly with her shimmering gown, showing how metallic tones can unify an entire red-carpet style.
