# 7 Quick Makeup Shortcuts for Busy Women That Save Time

Using makeup shortcuts can save you a lot of time in the morning. Spending ages putting on your makeup is fine when you're getting ready for a special night out, but it's not always practical when you have a long morning commute or need to get family ready for the day before heading to work. Fortunately, there are plenty of smart makeup shortcuts that allow you to look polished without sacrificing sleep. Try these practical beauty tips to streamline your morning routine and feel confident in no time.

## 1. Tinted Eyelashes

![Tinted Eyelashes](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/10/07042218_9313_600x825.jpg)

One of the best makeup shortcuts is to tint your eyelashes, which is remarkably easy to do safely at home. Tinting your lashes avoids common morning makeup mishaps, such as accidentally smudging mascara when you are rushing out the door. A quality lash tint lasts for up to six weeks and offers natural definition that mimics mascara. Pair your tinted lashes with a quick curl using an eyelash curler, and your eyes will look awake and refreshed with zero daily maintenance.

## 2. Perfect Your Routine

![Perfect Your Routine](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/10/05144838_4387_424x572.jpg)

Perfecting your daily makeup routine comes down to consistency and eliminating unnecessary steps. Aim to build a reliable five-minute routine that highlights your best features without cluttering your vanity. Reserve complex eyeshadow blending and intricate smoky eyes for occasions when you have extra time.

- Apply a light base or tinted moisturizer
- Add a quick swipe of blush or bronzer
- Finish with mascara and a hydrating lip gloss

## 3. Forget Foundation

![Forget Foundation](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/10/05145159_2981_484x788.jpg)

When you need to get ready in a hurry, skip heavy liquid foundation and opt for a sheer, hydrating alternative. The easiest option is a lightweight [tinted moisturizer](https://www.glossier.com/) formula with built-in broad-spectrum SPF to protect your skin from sun damage while evening out your complexion. If you have targeted redness or dark circles, a quick dab of cream concealer provides all the coverage you need without looking cakey.

## 4. Easy Eyes

![Easy Eyes](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/10/05145417_1301_600x764.jpg)

Keep your eye makeup minimal on busy mornings to save valuable time on shading and precise application. Defining your lash line with a smooth pencil and applying mascara is often all you need to frame your eyes. For an effortless look, a creamy eyeliner like the [L'Oréal Paris Color Riche Le Stylo](https://www.lorealparisusa.com/) in Silver Couture glides on quickly and provides instant depth without needing multiple shadow brushes. If you have already embraced [eyelash tinting](#1), your eye routine becomes even faster.

## 5. Brilliant Bronzer

![Brilliant Bronzer](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/10/05145607_2461_400x600.jpg)

Bronzer is an exceptionally versatile tool for giving your face warmth and dimension in seconds. A light sweep across the high points of your face—your forehead, cheekbones, and jawline—instantly awakens dull skin. Using a warm bronzer can replace both blush and contour for a sun-kissed finish. Just remember to apply with a fluffy brush and a light hand so the finish remains natural and seamless.

## 6. Lovely Lips

![Lovely Lips](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/10/07042312_1402_600x879.jpg)

Choose lip products in neutral, nude, or soft rose tones that fade gracefully throughout the day. Bold, highly pigmented lipsticks require precise application and frequent touch-ups, which can be high-maintenance during a busy schedule. A nourishing tinted balm or high-shine lip gloss is far easier to reapply on the go without requiring a mirror.

## 7. Marvelous Multitasking

![Marvelous Multitasking](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/10/05150130_3092_600x696.jpg)

Multitasking beauty products are essential for anyone wanting to streamline their cosmetics bag. Beyond combining hydration and coverage with a [tinted moisturiser](#3), look for dual-ended sticks or cream sticks that work on both lips and cheeks. Shopping at your favorite [beauty store](https://www.sephora.com/) for multi-use sticks allows you to cut down on the number of products you carry while creating a harmonious monochromatic color scheme in seconds.

Putting on your makeup does not have to take ages in the morning, and keeping things simple allows you to look polished while saving time for what matters most. Which single beauty product is your absolute non-negotiable when you are in a rush?
