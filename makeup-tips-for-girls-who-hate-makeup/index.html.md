# 8 Effortless Makeup Tips for Women Who Hate Wearing Makeup

If you're a girl who hates makeup or someone who rarely wears it, you are definitely not alone. Whether you feel like application is a massive time-suck, you hate the heavy feel of product on your skin, or you simply prefer a natural look, makeup for people who hate makeup should be low-stress and straightforward. If you've been asking yourself, "Why do I hate makeup?" or you are curious about simple makeup tips for girls who want a quick routine, take a look at these practical suggestions designed specifically for girls who don't wear makeup every day.

## 1. Why do You Hate It?

The top of my 'tips for girls who hate makeup' list is all about identifying exactly why you hate it. Is it a time-waste for you? Do you think it's pointless, or do you feel like hate wearing makeup comes down to sensory discomfort? These are all valid points, and who knows—maybe you don't need it at all! Makeup is just an optional enhancement to your own natural beauty, and not every girl needs it! Recognizing your personal reasons helps you decide if you want to explore [time-saving tricks](#2) or skip cosmetics entirely.

## 2. Time-Saving Tricks

If the time-suck is your biggest problem with cosmetics, why not take into account some time-saving tips? Makeup doesn't have to take forever to put on. In fact, you can streamline your routine in just about 5 to 10 minutes. A quick routine for someone who doesn't wear makeup might look like this:

- Apply mineral makeup or a light tinted moisturizer for sheer coverage
- Dab a bit of spot concealer under the eyes or on red areas
- Swipe on a coat of lightweight mascara and soft eyeliner

By keeping your steps minimal, you get a fresh look without spending half an hour in front of the mirror in the morning.

## 3. Start Simple

If you haven't worn makeup in a really long time and are thinking about getting back into it, start simple. The best makeup for someone who doesn't wear makeup is product that requires zero technical skill. Just try a hydrating tinted lip balm or go with simple mascara. These are easy entry points that won't take a lot of time or a lot of practice! If you find yourself asking why starting feels overwhelming, focus on just one feature at a time rather than a full face.

## 4. Go Light

Going with a lighter look is probably the best option when you really, really hate makeup. Start with just a dab of concealer, a dusting of translucent powder, and just a bit of soft eyeliner. Light neutral colors are also some of the best ways to save time on your makeup too, as they aren't as dramatic or unforgiving. If you want to refine your minimal routine, check out our tips on [starting simple](#3) with lightweight formulas.

| Product Type | Key Benefit | Effort Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tinted Moisturizer | Lightweight hydration and sheer coverage | Low |
| Clear Brow Gel | Keeps brows neat in five seconds | Minimal |
| Tinted Lip Balm | Adds subtle color without stickiness | Minimal |

## 5. Lip Gloss

If you hate lipstick (like me!), then don't wear it! Skip sticky or drying matte lipsticks and instead go with something that is going to feel much more comfortable, like a light lip gloss or hydrating lip oil. It's better to wear something that is not too sticky but still feels good on your lips. That's the best way to ensure that you are wearing a little bit of makeup, but not overdoing it!

## 6. Don't Wear It

I know makeup isn't for everyone, so if you hate it – don't wear it! Nobody is saying that you have to wear it, and I bet that you are all beautiful without it! If you're a girl who just really wants to go natural, don't worry about it! Natural skin with a solid skincare routine is gorgeous too!

## 7. Good Products

Giving makeup a chance is also an option, and having really good products is incredibly important. I'm not telling you to go out and buy a bunch of expensive luxury products, but don't go super cheap either. Go with medium-range and medium-price point makeup so that you get quality without breaking the bank. Popular beauty destinations like [Sephora](https://www.sephora.com) and [Ulta Beauty](https://www.ulta.com) offer great skin tints and multi-use sticks that simplify application for beginners.

## 8. Tutorials

Finally, if you hate makeup because you can't put it on correctly, tutorials are a great way to really learn all of the hottest tips and tricks! This is how I learned how to do all kinds of eyeliner tricks! There are a ton of easy makeup tutorials right on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com) that focus on quick, natural routines for people who prefer a minimal look.

So, do you still hate makeup? If so, why? Do you think that you need it? There are tons of benefits of makeup, but if you really hate it, that's okay too! It's all about finding what works best for you and your lifestyle.
