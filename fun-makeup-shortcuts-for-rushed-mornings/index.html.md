# 9 Smart & Easy Makeup Shortcuts for Rushed Mornings

Makeup shortcuts are a gift from above when you are trying to get out the door fast. Who doesn’t like clever tips and tricks to make a morning routine smoother and faster? Learning quick techniques can transform a stressful morning into a seamless beauty routine. Here are nine amazing makeup shortcuts for rushed mornings that will save you time without sacrificing your style.

| Shortcut | Best For | Time Saved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gel Liner Flame | Smudgy Eyeliner | 2 mins |
| White Base | Vibrant Eyeshadow | 3 mins |
| Spoon Wing | Perfect Cat-Eye | 5 mins |

## 1. Turn Normal Eyeliner into a Gel Liner

![Turn Normal Eyeliner into a Gel Liner](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/q1/j2/ehj95m04_389x278.jpg)

This is one of my favorite makeup shortcuts in the book! Sometimes, you need a creamy gel liner for a rich, intense look, but what if you don’t have one on hand? You can easily convert any regular pencil eyeliner into a gel formula by holding the tip under a lighter flame for just one second. Let it cool down for about 20 seconds, and then apply it for an instantly smoother, smudgier pencil application. If you enjoy versatile eye hacks, you can also check out our [spoon technique for winged liner](#3) for effortless precision.

## 2. Make Eyeshadow Pop

![Make Eyeshadow Pop](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/e7/ey/da4tr6tt_600x429.jpg)

It is frustrating when you apply a decent amount of eyeshadow, but the color payoff still looks muted. Packing on layer after layer often leads to a heavy, chalky finish that never looks good. The next time this happens, try covering your entire eyelid with white eyeliner or a white eyeshadow base first. This creates a vibrant canvas that makes any eyeshadow shade pop effortlessly.

## 3. Use a Spoon to Help Create a Winged Liner

![Use a Spoon to Help Create a Winged Liner](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/rr/xa/w2u6ky1q_450x323.jpg)

Winged liner can be notoriously difficult on rushed mornings, but using a simple kitchen spoon makes your routine much easier! Hold the stem of the spoon against the outer corner of your eye to draw a straight flick. Next, flip the spoon so the curved bowl faces outward, and use the rounded outer edge to guide your second line, creating a clean curved winged effect. To elevate your eye makeup further, pair this with our guide on how to [make eyeshadow pop](#2) before adding your liner.

## 4. Get an Instant Smoky Eye

![Get an Instant Smoky Eye](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/c1/gq/uf28d96s_600x493.jpg)

A classic smoky eye is a go-to evening look, but blending multiple shades from a shadow palette takes time when you are in a rush. A neat trick is to use a creamy shadow stick, such as [L'Oréal Paris](https://www.lorealparisusa.com) Color Riche Le Stylo smoky eye shadow, to draw a hashtag symbol (#) or parallel lines on the outer corner of your eyelid. Smudge and blend it inward using a sponge or blending brush for an instant, beautifully blended smoky eye in seconds.

## 5. Avoid Mascara Marks on Your Upper EyeLid

![Avoid Mascara Marks on Your Upper EyeLid](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/wh/yo/b69zqnt6_476x340.jpg)

Getting wet mascara smudges on your freshly painted upper eyelid is one of the most annoying mishaps when getting ready. Next time, try holding a clean spoon, business card, or plastic card right above your lash line against your skin. As you wiggle your mascara wand through your lashes, any excess residue coats the card instead of ruining your eye makeup.

## 6. Rescue Flaky Mascara

![Rescue Flaky Mascara](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/d7/s9/0k8h12xf_539x450.jpg)

If you wear contact lenses or have sensitive eyes, dried-out mascara can be a nightmare. Mascara tubes frequently dry up before the recommended three-month replacement period. Adding a few drops of sterile [Bausch + Lomb](https://www.bausch.com) saline solution in mascara tubes revitalizes the formula instantly, making it smooth and as good as new without causing irritation. For additional mascara application tips, check out our trick to [prevent mascara marks on your eyelid](#5).

## 7. Get the Perfect Cupid’s Bow

![Get the Perfect Cupid’s Bow](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/of/vt/xp19sxy1_494x353.jpg)

Bold red lips remain one of the most timeless beauty trends. However, defining a symmetrical cupid’s bow can be tricky. The easiest method is to take a sharp lip liner matching your lipstick shade and draw a clean 'X' right at the center of your upper lip’s cupid’s bow. This gives you a clear guideline to trace your full lip color effortlessly.

Here is a quick checklist for quick morning lip prep:

- Exfoliate gently with a damp washcloth
- Apply hydrating lip balm before liner
- Outline the cupid's bow with an X pattern

## 8. Blot Your Face and Get Rid of Oil

![Blot Your Face and Get Rid of Oil](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/m6/wc/he20hz0z_592x429.jpg)

Even if you have dry skin, shine and oil can build up throughout the day, especially in the T-zone. Since not everyone carries dedicated blotting papers everywhere, you can use clean paper toilet seat covers in a restroom to blot away excess oil without disturbing your foundation.

## 9. Apply Eyelash Glue Cleanly

![Apply Eyelash Glue Cleanly](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/a7/oe/itxewqkh_600x429.jpg)

Applying adhesive directly from the tube to false lash bands often creates a mess. Instead, dab a small drop of glue onto the tip of a bobby pin or toothpick, then lightly trace it along the lash band. Wait about 20 to 30 seconds for the glue to become tacky before placing the strip onto your lash line for a secure, seamless hold.

These makeup shortcuts are simple hacks that make morning beauty routines faster and stress-free. Whether you are fixing dried mascara or perfecting a cat-eye, incorporating these quick tricks will keep you looking polished on busy mornings. What are your favorite go-to makeup shortcuts?
