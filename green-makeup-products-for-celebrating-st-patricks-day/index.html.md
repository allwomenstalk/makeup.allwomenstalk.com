If you don't want to wear green clothing, how about trying some green makeup? You can avoid the pinching fingers of your friends, family and co-workers and look gorgeous at the same time. Quick tips for wearing green makeup effortlessly:

- Focus on one feature at a time—either a bold eye or a festive nail polish.

- Pair bright green tones with neutral bronzes and earthy brown transition shades.

- Use waterproof formulas to keep your festive look locked in place all day.
