# Latte Makeup: How to Get the Warm, Coffee\-Toned Look

Latte makeup is a warm, coffee-toned look that uses related shades across the eyes, cheeks and lips. Bronzer gives the face soft dimension; brown shadow and liner define the eyes; and a nude-brown lip ties it together. The secret is choosing depths that suit your skin, then blending so the colors feel connected rather than flat.

## What is latte makeup?

Think caramel, cinnamon, toffee and espresso, adjusted to your own complexion. Celebrity makeup artist Brandy Allen describes the look as keeping eyes, cheeks and lips in one warm color family. Smashbox global pro makeup artist Lori Taylor Davis says it works by layering different depths, rather than applying the same brown everywhere. Both artists shared their guidance with [Cosmopolitan](https://www.cosmopolitan.com/style-beauty/beauty/a74286423/latte-makeup-trend/).

The look returns when the weather cools, according to Cosmopolitan. You can make it barely there for daytime or deepen the eye for evening. You do not need a particular palette or brand: a bronzer, a couple of complementary shadows, blush, brown liner and a lip color in the same family are enough.

## How to do latte makeup in five steps

### 1. Start with your usual base

Use the skin base you already like. A light layer lets your complexion show through and leaves room for bronzer to add warmth. If you prefer no foundation, the rest of the look still works. The goal is a soft finish, not an opaque brown mask.

### 2. Build warmth with bronzer

Davis calls bronzer central to the look. Choose a depth that shows up on your skin without looking orange or ashy. Sweep and blend it across the temples, cheekbones and outer edges of the face. A cream formula can make soft edges easier, but your usual powder works too. If bronzer has always seemed tricky on fair skin, see our [pale-skin bronzer guide](https://makeup.allwomenstalk.com/how-to-pull-off-bronzer-when-youre-pale/).

### 3. Add blush with some depth

A blush keeps the look from becoming one flat shade. Cosmopolitan suggests soft rose on fair skin and deeper red or brown tones on deeper skin. For a longer-wearing finish, Allen suggests blending cream blush first, then pressing powder blush over it. Start lightly and add color where you want it.

### 4. Blend a coffee-toned eye

Place a lighter caramel or warm neutral over the lid, then blend a deeper chocolate or espresso shade through the crease and outer corner. Keep the transition diffused. A touch of gold shimmer can add light if you like it. Smudge a brown pencil close to the lashes; Allen recommends it for a softer line than black. Brown mascara continues that softness, while black mascara adds contrast. Choose the one you prefer.

### 5. Finish with a nude-brown lip

Trace your natural lip shape with a caramel or deep-brown pencil, depending on your coloring, and soften the edge. Add a nude gloss or comfortable lip color from the same tonal family. Our [lip-liner mistakes guide](https://makeup.allwomenstalk.com/lip-liner-mistakes-thatll-ruin-your-look/) can help if the outline looks too sharp. The lip should balance the eyes, not compete with them.

If a suggested shade turns orange or gray on you, change the undertone while keeping the overall look softly coordinated. On deeper skin, a light beige product may disappear or leave a cast; on fair skin, an espresso shade can work beautifully as a small accent rather than an all-over wash.

## How to keep latte makeup from looking muddy

Use at least two depths on the eyes and blend their edges. Keep the bronzer softly sculpted instead of drawing a hard stripe. Add one point of light, such as a small gold sheen on the lid or gloss on the lips. Step back from the mirror in daylight before adding more brown: you should still see your own skin and the separate shapes of your eyes, cheeks and lips.

## FAQ

### What is latte makeup?

It is a soft makeup look built from related coffee-inspired neutrals across the eyes, cheeks and lips, with bronzer supplying much of the warmth.

### Does latte makeup work on deep skin?

Yes. Choose bronzer, shadow, blush and lip shades with enough depth to show on your complexion; deep reds, chocolate browns and espresso accents can create the same coordinated effect.

### Do I need brown mascara?

No. Brown mascara keeps the eye soft and tonal, while black mascara gives more contrast. Both can work.

### Is latte makeup the same as a brown smoky eye?

No. A smoky eye focuses on the lids. Latte makeup coordinates the eye with bronzed cheeks, blush and a related nude-brown lip.
