# The 9 Best 60\-Second Makeup Tips for a Quick, Radiant Look

You love to step out of the house looking and feeling great, but you are stuck looking for 60-second makeup tips that actually work! We all have those mornings when you don’t have time for a full beauty routine before hurrying out the door. You honestly don’t need a heavy application to look pulled together; just a few quick strokes and smart makeup tips will instantly revitalize your appearance. Whether you are looking for the best makeup tips for over 60 or simply searching for the best products for a 60-second makeup routine, looking amazing does not require an hour in front of the mirror. Take a look at these top 9 one-minute makeup tips that will have you rushing out the door on time and looking fabulous!

## 1. Metallic Shadow for Extra Flair

Touch up your eyes with a little metallic shadow, being sure to select a shade that brings a spark to your eyes and complements your natural eye color. The metallic eye shadow should stand out to give the subtle impression of a twinkle. Brush a tiny dab of your favorite shade into the inner corners of your eyes to really make them pop! This is one of my favorite 60-second makeup tips of all time, easily found at beauty retailers like [Sephora](https://www.sephora.com).

## 2. Cover up Fatigue Signs

After a tiring day or a long night, you might notice your face looking a bit dull or tired. Perk up instantly by dabbing concealer onto the inner corners of your eyes, alongside your nose, and right under your lower lashes. Next, brush a touch of bronzer onto your cheeks to add warmth and definition. To complement this fatigue-busting trick, pair it with [strategic highlighter placement](#6) for an all-over healthy glow. This process is one of the fastest ways to appear energized and wide awake during a busy workweek!

## 3. Wonders of Blush

Apply your favorite cream or powder blush to the apples of your cheeks; the pop of color imparts instant liveliness to your face, significantly reducing the need for additional makeup. A few light brush strokes along your cheeks, and even a soft dusting on your chin and nose to add warmth, will brighten your entire face. This 60-second makeup tip brings your complexion to life in seconds!

- Keep a versatile multi-stick blush in your handbag for quick touch-ups.
- Use clean fingertips for seamless, natural blending on the go.
- Focus on bright eyes and defined lips to complete a fast look.

## 4. Outline for Perfection

Find a pretty lip liner similar to your natural lip color and trace the contours of your lips, then smooth on a matching lip stain or lipstick for a polished finish. You will get an enhanced pout that is great for any occasion. You can even top it off with a touch of gloss for extra shine and dimension, available from favorite spots like [Ulta Beauty](https://www.ulta.com).

## 5. Trust Loose Foundation

Rather than heavy creams or liquid foundations, dust your face with a light loose powder foundation for a natural, fresh-faced finish. Powder foundation is comparatively easier to use for quick mid-day re-touches and instantly sweeps away excess shine across your T-zone. This 60-second makeup tip is one I rely on daily, and it works beautifully every single time!

## 6. Highlighter is a Time-saver

A soft, translucent highlighter emphasizes the best features of your face through a subtle shimmer and healthy glow. Stroke highlighter along your cheekbones to accentuate your bone structure, sweep a little under your brow bone, onto your eyelids, and even onto the center of your lips. If you want to keep your skin fresh without heavy layers, balance this with a [light loose foundation](#5). With an added sweep of mascara, your look is effortlessly complete.

## 7. Taupe Eye Shadow is Magnificent

Taupe is a remarkably striking and versatile shade that flatters virtually every skin tone. Gently sweep a wash of taupe eyeshadow over your eyelids with your finger. It serves as a wonderful base for brown, navy, or black eyeliner, making your eye color pop instantly. Cream shadow sticks like [L'Oréal Paris](https://www.lorealparisusa.com) Color Riche Le Stylo in shades like Conquest or Violet Amour make this step completely effortless whether creating a daytime or evening look.

| Eye Color | Complementary Shadow | Fast Application Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green Eyes | Warm Purple & Violet | Sweep along outer corners |
| Blue Eyes | Rich Taupe & Bronze | Smudge lightly along lash line |
| Brown Eyes | Navy & Metallic Shimmer | Apply as an easy cream wash |

## 8. Colour-coded Eye Shadows for Vibrancy

One way to intensify your eye color is choosing a combo of shadows and liners in hues that complement your iris. Green eyes are best highlighted with purple tones, while blue eyes sparkle with warm browns and violet. Many eyeshadow quads are specifically color-coded for your eye shade, making quick selection foolproof. For instant face definition before applying shadow, make sure to frame your face with [well-defined brows](#9).

## 9. Defined Brows

Clearly defined eyebrows frame your features and instantly create a polished appearance. Using an eyebrow pencil or tinted gel to fill in sparse areas creates graceful arches that structure your whole face. Filling in your eyebrows to add shape and definition is an essential 60-second makeup tip that transforms your entire face in seconds!

Test out these 60-second makeup tips and see how easily they fit into your morning routine! There is no need to spend half an hour in front of the mirror when you are running late; in just a few minutes, you can look completely put together and fabulous. Do you have a favorite one-minute makeup hack that saves you in a pinch? Share your tips in the comments below!
