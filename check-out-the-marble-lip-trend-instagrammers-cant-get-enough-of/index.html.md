# 20 Marble Lip Trend Looks Instagram Is Obsessed With \(And How to Recreate Them\)

Takeaway: What is the marble lip trend

There is a stunning beauty trend taking over Instagram that makeup artists and beauty lovers are obsessed with: marble lips! Professional artists are recreating a realistic marble stone finish directly on their lips, and the best part is that it is simple enough for you to achieve at home. Looks range from a classic monochrome marble to vibrant gemstone swirls with metallic accents. Popular digital publications like [Bored Panda](https://www.boredpanda.com) have shown how easy this look is to recreate: simply apply a smooth base coat to your lips and paint small veins of color using a thin detail brush. The rest is up to your imagination! To get creative makeup inspiration for the newest marble lip trend, check out the 20 incredible looks below!

Key Essentials for Perfect Marble Lips:

- Opaque Liquid Base: White or nude liquid lipstick creates a solid stone canvas.
- Fine Detailer Brush: A thin nail art or eyeliner brush allows precise marble veining.
- High-Shine Clear Gloss: Seals the design with a dimensional, polished stone finish.
