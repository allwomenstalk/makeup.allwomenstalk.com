# How to Wear Red Eyeliner \(Without Looking Like You Haven't Slept\): A Colored\-Liner Guide

To wear red eyeliner without looking tired, keep it on your top lash line, start with burgundy or rust rather than true red, and tone everything else down. Makeup artist Karol Rodriguez suggests a softer pinky-berry blush, skin-toned lips and, if you want more, burgundy mascara on the lower lashes.

Red liner sounds like a Halloween-only move, but it’s been showing up on red carpets for months. The trick to making it wearable is placement, shade and balance. Get those right, and the worst-case scenario, as [Allure](https://www.allure.com/story/zendaya-rihanna-maisie-williams-red-eyeliner-trend) puts it, “is that you do end up looking like a glamorous vampire, and that’s pretty awesome, too.”

## Why red eyeliner is everywhere right now

Allure reports that red eyeliner has been building momentum since spring, with celebrities wearing it in very different ways:

- **Rihanna** wore a precise stripe of vivid ruby along her lower waterlines at the Matières Fécales show in Paris.

- **Maisie Williams** had burgundy tones smudged under her lower lashes at the European premiere of _Practical Magic 2_.

- **Zendaya** tried rust, which Allure calls “the gateway shade,” at the Rome premiere of _The Drama_, and wore a true-red pencil all around her eyes at a photo call for _Spider-Man: Brand New Day_.

Three very different looks, one big question: can you wear it on a regular Tuesday? Makeup artist Karol Rodriguez says yes.

## Prep your lids

Start with a smooth base so the color looks intentional. Spanish makeup artist Natalia Belda told [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com/article/red-eyeliner) her universal tip: “first neutralize the eyelid with a small amount of concealer applied with a proper brush, then gently blend it out using soft taps of the fingers.” An eye primer can help the liner last, and a touch of translucent powder on the lid can stop it from sliding.

## Pick your red: rust, burgundy or true red

Not every red is equal, and this is where the “tired” look usually comes from. Belda warns that “very bright or true reds can exaggerate signs of fatigue or eye redness and may even make pink-toned dark circles more noticeable.” She prefers reds tempered with brown: “think more burgundy than tomato.”

## Keep it on the top lid

Rodriguez’s most practical rule, as told to Allure: “Keep it on the top lid.” Most of us already have some redness or pinkness along the lower waterline, so adding bright red there can make you look like you’ve been staring at your laptop for hours.

Lining the full eye, top and bottom, is still an option, but treat it as the bold version for nights out or Halloween rather than an everyday look.

**A quick eye-safety note:** use products labeled safe for use around the eyes, and stop using a liner if your eyes become irritated.

## Balance the rest of your face

Red liner gives you the impact of a red lip in a much less expected place, so let it be the only statement. “If you’re doing a red liner, play down the blush a little bit,” Rodriguez told Allure. “Keep it in that same pinky-berry-red family, but make it less intense.” Keep your lips skin-toned.

## Three ways to wear red eyeliner

- **The precise top line.** A slim line of rust or burgundy along the upper lash line, with or without a small wing. This is the easiest everyday version.

- **The smudged burgundy.** Softly smudge burgundy liner or shadow under the lower lashes, as Williams did, and keep the top lid neutral. It looks lived-in rather than graphic.

- **The full graphic red.** True red on the waterline, as Rihanna wore it, or all around the eye, like Zendaya’s photo-call look. Save it for evenings, events and Halloween, and keep the rest of your makeup minimal.

## Can red liner make tired eyes look brighter?

Belda thinks it can, if you choose well. She told Vogue that reddish tones are often softer than black or brown and can “refresh the eyes, help neutralize under-eye darkness, and soften a tired look.” She’s clear that they don’t literally rejuvenate, and that the right shade depends on your undertone, eye color and the kind of dark circles you have. That’s one makeup artist’s experience, so try it and see what your face says.

## FAQ

### How do you wear red eyeliner without looking tired?

Keep it on your top lash line, choose a burgundy or rust shade rather than a bright true red, and soften the rest of your makeup with a pinky-berry blush and skin-toned lips.

### Should red eyeliner go on the top lid or the waterline?

For everyday wear, the top lid. Makeup artist Karol Rodriguez notes that the lower waterline already has some natural redness, so bright red there can make eyes look tired. Save the waterline for a bolder look, and only use eye-safe products.

### What shade of red eyeliner is most flattering?

Most people find burgundy or rust easiest to wear. Makeup artist Natalia Belda prefers reds tempered with brown, “more burgundy than tomato,” and warns that very bright reds can exaggerate fatigue.

### What blush and lipstick go with red eyeliner?

A softer blush in the pinky-berry family and skin-toned lips, according to Rodriguez. For more color, add burgundy mascara to your lower lashes.

### Can red eyeliner hide dark circles?

It may help a little. Belda says well-chosen reddish tones can help neutralize under-eye darkness, but very bright reds can make pink-toned dark circles more noticeable. It won’t hide them completely.
